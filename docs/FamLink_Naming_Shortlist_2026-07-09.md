## Why rename

- **Google Family Link** (familylink.google.com) — parental-control product with a huge install base in the adjacent space. "FamLink" can never win spoken referral or search against it.
- **FamLink is Washington State's child-welfare (CPS/foster care) case-management system** (DCYF, ~233k client families). Bad association for a custody-adjacent family product.
- Root also occupied by thefamlink.org (nonprofit), famlink.care, famlink.com (genealogy site), and FamLink forensic-genetics software. The *root* is the problem, not the TLD — variants won't fix it.
- Supersedes the Competitive Dossier §G assessment ("FamLink is serviceable") — that note evaluated warmth, not collision.

## Positioning constraint (decided)

Steve, 2026-07-09: *"I want something that provides genuine utility but with warmth and belonging at its core."* → Name lane: **a warm object/idiom that inherently does coordination work** (the Hearth/Skylight trick applied to connection). Off-limits roots (competitor/TM collisions): hearth, cozy/cozi, nest, skylight, fam-, jam.

## The three tests

1. **Radio test** — hear it once, spell it right, find it. No homophones, no numerals.
2. **Text-message test** — growth loop = invite links SMS'd/emailed to strangers (guest RSVP, cross-family invites, W3b SMS onboarding). The domain is the first brand touch; it must look trustworthy in a text. Exact-match .com NOT required; clean .app/.family is fine at launch.
3. **Warmth with a job** — warm word that inherently means coordinating / connecting / making room.
